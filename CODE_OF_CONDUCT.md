# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions) is expected to be
respectful and constructive. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination
and publishing other people's private information are not.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, comments and issues that do not follow
these rules, and may ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

To report a problem, email the maintainer at the address on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/AristideDongo). Reports are handled confidentially.

This text is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
